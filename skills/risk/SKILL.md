# Risk and events

Analyze downside, not generic company quality.

1. Start with dated Marked events, filings and corporate actions. Establish what was known at `as_of`; if no triggering disclosure exists, say so.
2. Measure the exposed financial channels: revenue and margin sensitivity, operating cash conversion, receivables and inventory, borrowings, finance costs, liquidity and interest coverage.
3. Check promoter ownership, pledge, FII/FPI and DII changes for alignment, funding pressure or governance signals.
4. Check price before and after the event when candles are available. Describe raw reaction only; do not call it abnormal without a benchmark.
5. Separate risks into current evidence, watch conditions and unsupported possibilities. Never turn a plausible scenario into a fact.
6. Rank material risks by severity, likelihood and time horizon using qualitative labels unless Marked supplies inputs for arithmetic.
7. For each leading risk state the evidence IDs, transmission channel, observable trigger, expected direction of impact and what would invalidate it.
8. Finish with near-term catalysts, the most important unknowns and an explicit data-gap list.

Do not provide guaranteed outcomes, invented probabilities, price targets or personalized advice.
