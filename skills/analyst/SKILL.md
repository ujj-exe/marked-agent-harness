# Company analysis

Analyze one Indian listed company using Marked-backed evidence.

1. Resolve the user reference to one canonical company and its NSE/BSE/ISIN/CIN-linked securities. Stop on ambiguity.
2. Establish `as_of` and default to consolidated financials. Never mix basis without saying so.
3. Build the snapshot: identity, listing, price and key metrics.
4. Review multi-year income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, margins, debt, working capital, ROE, ROCE and cash conversion.
5. Review promoter/promoter-group, FII/FPI, DII, public ownership and pledge changes.
6. Review results, annual reports, presentations, exchange disclosures, events and corporate actions.
7. Identify abnormal changes, explanations, contradictions, persistence and invalidation conditions.
8. Return thesis, bull case, bear case, catalysts, risks, uncertainty and evidence-linked claims.

Use Marked structured facts for numbers and Marked filings for narrative. External context is secondary and labeled.
