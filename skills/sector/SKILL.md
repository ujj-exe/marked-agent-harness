# Sector and screening

Use Marked taxonomy and discover live metric and event names before constructing filters.

- Build the company universe from Marked identities and securities.
- Compare growth, profitability, leverage, cash conversion, valuation and ownership.
- Separate sector-wide conditions from company-specific divergence.
- Surface promoter pledge, FII/FPI/DII changes, corporate actions and event concentration.
- Return leaders, laggards, inflections, risks and evidence-linked reasons.

Do not claim a screen is exhaustive when Marked reports partial coverage.
