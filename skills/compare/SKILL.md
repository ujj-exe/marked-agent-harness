- Normalize fiscal period, consolidated/standalone basis, unit, currency and metric definition.
- Compare growth, EBITDA/PAT margins, ROE, ROCE, debt, net debt, cash conversion, EPS, valuation where available, ownership, pledge, filings and events.
- Call out leader, laggard, inflection, balance-sheet strength, earnings momentum, valuation tension and biggest risk.
- Missing metrics are unavailable, not zero. Return mixed or insufficient evidence when ranking is unsupported.
